Q2  OPERATORS
 Production (CNativeEngine3D/CNativeBoundary3D/CNativeCavity3D): Refine = exact on-edge split, certified edge-cavity
 insertion (InsertEdgeCavity), wall refit/PlaceSplitPatch, ReconstructHeightStar, repair fallback; Coarsen = CollapseVertex
 (directed vertex-star contraction); Repair = Reconnect (face 2->3, edge 3->2 / 4->4 to an existing apex), MoveVertex,
 RepairMetricConnectivity (bounded 2/3/4-tet flips, interior vertex removal, metric-gradient vertex movement inside a
 FIXED cavity skin, <= 128 cells, loss sum max(0,1-q/.2)^2+max(0,1-J/.05)^2). Missing: general edge removal (shell n > 4
 -> 2n-4), a mesh-wide quality pass whose steps may leave cells below the gate, sliver collapse as an optimization step,
 any layer/sheet operator (insert a whole layer of points at one metric height over a wall patch).
 Every production metric change calls Shape(measured): ALL fresh cells must already satisfy q >= .20, J >= .05 (absolute,
 for Refine, Coarsen and Repair alike). The harness Admit8 uses min(old cavity min, .20/.05).
 Harness V (PhaseV11): per long edge SplitEdge / InsertEdgeCavity / wall variants, each also tried with
 RepairMetricConnectivity on its own fresh cavity (skin fixed = old edge star); per bad cell RepairMetricConnectivity on
 its vertex-star union; ApexMove / ColumnSplit in columns. No collapse, no flips across the cavity skin, no smoothing pass.
 Accepted (120 s): root_te SplitEdge 250, InsertEdgeCavity 231, +repair 223, ColumnSplit 2, bad-cell repair 0 (the first
 sweep never reached it); m6_le 731 accepted / 6764 refused (repair 144); diag600 root_te 3322 accepted (repair 164) vs
 14537 refused. In 120 s V completes 1 sweep on root/tip TE.
 Literature: Loseille-Lohner 2009 (Prove/Papers) insert on an edge if every new tet has POSITIVE VOLUME, then a relaxed
 quality tolerance (new worst <= qr x old worst with qr > 1, lowered to 1 over the passes, plus a global qa) "to get out
 of local minima"; they name exactly our failure: one-edge-at-a-time refinement in a strongly anisotropic field leaves the
 orthogonal sizes worse and "edges acting as a barrier". Swaps and point smoothing follow. MMG3D: split/collapse/swap/move
 interleaved, splits checked essentially for validity, quality restored by later swaps/moves. Marcum 2014 (AFLR):
 point generation then local reconnection to convergence each pass, smoothing at the end. Loseille 2014: cavity insertion
 with cavity enlargement (Alg. 1) and a height check against slivers. None applies an absolute per-operation gate.

CAUSE (summary). V refines a mesh whose only wall-normal resolution is the h0 row, directly under cells 10-1000 h0 tall,
in a target graded by 1.2 per layer. Each single-edge split of such a cell creates needles/slivers (q ~ .05, J ~ .005)
because the orthogonal sizes are not touched (Loseille-Lohner's barrier configuration); a per-operation q/J gate refuses
them (98 % of production metric trials; 67 % of all V work in the 600 s root_te run), and the accepted splits fill the 50-800 h0 range while 3-25 h0 stays
empty, so the long-edge count plateaus (2163 -> 2400 while cells x4). The cut fixture and the split certificate are
secondary. Tubes differ: the volume target is reachable (Q3) and local swap/smooth fixes 14/20 refusals; there the first
row/column interaction (unrowed faces, column cells) is the blocker.

RECOMMENDATION (user decision 2026-10-23: q >= .20 / J >= .05 only as the FINAL acceptance check; intermediate steps need
validity only; then an optimization pass reaches the final gates). Assessment: necessary and supported by the data
(100 % of floor refusals are valid; the literature does the same), but NOT sufficient alone on M6: (b) shows local
swap/smooth after single-edge splits reaches the absolute gate in 0-2/20 M6 samples, and Q1 shows the wall-normal layers
are missing. The plan therefore needs four connected parts:
 1. Generation with validity only: Refine/Coarsen/Repair proposals accepted on positive volume (with a sliver-height
    lower bound as in Loseille 2014, e.g. J > 1e-3, to keep the optimizer's start finite), geometry/facet authority,
    first-height and footprint rules, unchanged first-row cells, size criterion (2D split rule); no q/J floor. Production
    Shape(measured) and Admit8's floor become a final-pass-only check (one RefineAcceptance-like switch, A/B kept).
 2. BL growth layers (new operator, the main M6 gap): from the first-row lid, insert points along each lid vertex's
    BL direction (ApexLP / production column direction) at the metric-unit heights h_k = h0 g^k of the D1-free target
    until the local volume size is met, as one sheet transaction over a wall patch (all columns of the patch at the same
    k, coned per column like R2), validity-gated; general (driven by the target and the requested wall height, no case
    switch). This replaces ~17-29 rounds of single-edge splits that the shape barrier blocks.
 3. Optimization pass (mesh-wide, iterated to a fixed point or budget): edge/face swaps 2-3, 3-2, 4-4 and general edge
    removal n -> 2n-4 (new) across ANY cavity skin; metric smoothing of free interior vertices and of surface vertices on
    their facets (RepairMetricConnectivity's movement, freed from the per-cavity fixed skin); sliver removal by collapse
    of the shortest metric edge or vertex removal (CollapseVertex / interior removal); interleaved with split/collapse
    passes as in MMG/Loseille (qr relaxation lowered to 1 over passes). First-row cells and lid vertices frozen except
    certified ApexMove; height checked on every step.
 4. Final acceptance unchanged: all edges <= 1.8, all q >= .20, J >= .05, first height within 10 %, complete first row.
 ACCEPTANCE CONDITIONS of the change: (i) m6_root_te, m6_tip_te, m6_le complete under the unchanged final gates in a
 bounded serial budget, with the 3-25 h0 band populated at the target layer count (+/- 2 layers); (ii) tube_root_x,
 two_tube_y, annulus complete (needs the existing unrowed-face issue too); (iii) production volume-only Refine (Q3 test)
 completes on m6_root_te; (iv) no first-row cell or wall height changes outside certified moves (height failures 0);
 (v) unit3d/unit2d/metric/mesh3d/airfoil unchanged except intended expectations; (vi) the optimizer's non-convergence is
 reported per region (residual bad/long cells with their distance band), never hidden by relaxed gates.
 RISKS: (d1) optimization does not converge in strongly anisotropic regions (median old-ring q .15-.18 already; LL09
 needs gradation + many passes): mitigate with the layer operator (aligned cells first), metric gradation (ADAP_HGRAD
 1.3 is configured; whether the composed BL target is gradation-limited is not verified here) and bounded passes with explicit residual reporting; (d2) BL cells damaged: freeze first-row cells,
 lid vertices and their heights in every generation/optimization step (Shape is no longer the guard; height + footprint
 + positivity are), allow only certified ApexMove there; (d3) validity-only generation can create near-degenerate slivers
 that no swap repairs: keep a minimum-J/height floor at 1e-3 (validity, not quality) and run sliver removal first in the
 optimization; (d4) cost: more cells/work per sweep; bound by work/time and report.
