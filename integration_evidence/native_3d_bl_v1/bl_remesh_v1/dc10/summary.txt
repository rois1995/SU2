WHAT BLOCKS AN ACCEPTED MESH (single-lift baseline): the volume transition V. Remaining long edges are interior
(1185/1593 on root/tip) and bad cells are outside columns (233/179) plus crease-adjacent columns; V finishes 0-1
sweeps in 120 s and its refinements near creases are refused mainly by "refine: no strict changed-edge excess
reduction" and "D floor". The D1 wedge size h0 + ln(1.3) r around a crease cannot be reached from the coarse input
volume by greedy local steps that must each lower the summed squared excess (a single edge split near the crease
adds more long point-to-link edges than it removes) - the same certificate that refuses the fan. This is a
construction-strategy limit (admission potential vs strongly graded target), not a local defect.
