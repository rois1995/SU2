perf1: production performance pass on the native 3D BL metric evaluation (base 1899eccff9). Hashes: provenance.json.
All runs serial, nice 19, OMP_NUM_THREADS=1, MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2, with 4 foreign FSI MPI ranks loading the host.

Changes
 1. SU2Native3D::FusedMultiplyAdd (CNativeFma.hpp, body in CNativeMetric3D.cpp of the strict no-fast-math library):
    glibc ldbl-96 fmal algorithm (Dekker product, Knuth sum, round to odd) with the odd rounding emulated from the exact
    TwoSum error instead of fesetround/fetestexcept; std::fma outside a conservative exponent guard and on non-x87
    long double. Replaces every long-double std::fma in CBoundaryLayerMetric.cpp, CNativeChordEnvelope.hpp,
    CNativePredicates3D.cpp (Measure), CNativeMetric3D.cpp. Declared const/leaf/nothrow + noexcept: with an opaque or
    const-only declaration the fast-math inline ChordEnvelope::Add/Cuts were scheduled differently and 758/971 D2
    witness lengths changed (up to 9e-7 rel.) although the kernel itself was bit-identical (bisection variants A-F:
    item 3 alone, libm-switched kernel, HEAD BL metric, HEAD envelope); with nothrow+leaf every length is bit-identical.
 2. FrozenField::BalancedSplitPoint: the whole-edge EdgeLength also inverts its converged panel partition (Kronrod-15
    prefix + safeguarded Newton inside the panel holding the fraction; retired depth-20 panels kept) for a start
    parameter; an 8-step seeded attempt, then the unchanged original 48-step iteration from the plain fraction. Every
    returned point is certified by the same |L(a,p) - f L(a,b)| <= 1e-8 f L(a,b) check against its own prefix integral.
 3. CBoundaryLayerMetric::EvaluateImpl: a 3D non-authority wall with a chord-owned facet skips the ADT nearest-element
    descent and within-distance scan whose result the old code overwrote with the owner (identical sample).
 4. CNativeCavity3D.cpp Add()/Surface(): two-entry reserve on first insertion of link/adjacency/edge-owner vectors
    (vector<unsigned long> reallocation in cavity validation was ~7% of DC-8 V samples after items 1-3).
    EigenDecomposition (10% self after) is in IntersectMetrics/Spectral and cannot change without changing results;
    unpinned point-query ADT descents (7.9%) come from FrozenField::Query compositions (no chord ownership exists).
