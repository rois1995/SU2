6. RECOMMENDATION
-----------------
Top 3 cost centres:
  C1 uncached independent audit: ~4.35 ms/cell (projected ~3100 s here, > 4x the 720 s reserve) - killed the run.
     The cached Final at every V sweep end and after V is the same scan (~1M metric edge integrations).
  C2 V sweep structure: one sweep = 5205 s with fixed fractions of the whole remaining budget, a full-mesh long-edge
     scan, all refinement variants admitted per edge (Admit12 ~23 ms each in the cut) and an optimizer slice >= 40 %;
     attempt cap 9480/sweep against 158.8k long edges -> no convergence possible in any budget.
  C3 R2 on 38.8k wall faces (~60 ms/face, estimated ~1000-1600 s), plus W evaluating Option-A on a wall that needs
     no change.
Top 3 defect causes:
  D1 G stops after 0-1 layers (clearance/validity refusals against the coarse volume above the columns): the BL band
     3-300 h0 keeps coarse vertices -> 130k long edges in 10-300 h0 and the 10-100 h0 cone slivers (77 % of bad).
  D2 89 unrowed wall faces at the tip/TE corner (three boundary planes) -> 89 height failures -> the event is
     rejected regardless of everything else.
  D3 TE base corners and near wake (40.7k bad, 57.8k long, 41 % of the cells): first row + corner fans with no
     coordinated column/volume construction there; LE: coarse wall facets under the thin row (no surface/metric
     reconciliation at the LE, W changed nothing).
Smallest set of changes for one valid full-wing x32 cycle in 30-60 min serial:
  1 (speed, C1) intermediate events (final_cycle 0) decide acceptance by exactly what the rule needs: exact
    orientation of every cell + the height rule on the wall faces (seconds, uncached, independent); shape/length
    deficits are reported from the cached gates, labelled as cached. The full uncached audit is run only on the final
    cycle, and its cost goes into the budget: node-metric memo local to the audit (fresh, still independent of the
    construction caches), one edge set built once (sorted vector, not std::set), and the reserve sized from the
    measured ms/cell x cells.
  2 (robustness, D1, also removes most of C2's work) make G clear its own room: when a layer step is refused
    because the volume above is too close, remove the obstructing non-column volume vertices inside the column's
    envelope up to the BL thickness (cavity re-triangulation of the volume above the stack, existing SplitInsertion /
    cavity operators, Admit12 validity) and retry, instead of stopping the column. The layers then supply the
    10-300 h0 vertices that V cannot create by edge splits at 9480 attempts per 5205 s.
  3 (robustness, D2) at three-boundary-plane vertices (tip/TE corner) R2 must row every wall face or V's first-row
    route must be guaranteed for the unrowed ones (now limited to 2 tries per face and .1R per sweep): log and test the
    89 faces of this mesh as a fixed repro (extract a cut around x 1.14, y 1.197).
  4 (speed, C2) V budget per sweep sized from the work (attempt cap from the current long-edge count, not the input
    cell count; optimizer only over cells touched in the sweep; long-edge list and gate counts maintained by Apply
    deltas instead of a full rescan per sweep), and a hard V cap for intermediate events (e.g. 15 min) since their
    acceptance does not need the final gates.
  With 1 + 3 alone the current state would already pass the intermediate rule (no inverted cell; heights fixed) in
  ~ 30 (pre-V) + 15 (capped V) + ~2 (audit) min; 2 and 4 are what makes the next cycles converge towards the final
  gates. Open doubts: the W/R1/R2/G split is estimated, not measured (rerun with the per-phase timing printed to
  stdout - JsonStage seconds and the perf table flushed after each phase - would settle it); the region thresholds
  (5 % chord/span) are conventions.
