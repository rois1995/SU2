What this change does: DC-15 adds independent intermediate acceptance, first-row completion, and clearance recovery. The final cycle retains the uncached audit and final gates.

1. **Major — Cavity completion can invalidate column registries.** [BLEvent15.inl:104](/media/rausa/4TB/SU2_Versions/SU2_NativeIntegrated/SU2_CFD/include/adaptation/native_bl3d/BLEvent15.inl:104) and lines 116–127 exclude other first children, but allow the cavity to consume column cells and registered lids. Coning removes vertices internal to that cavity; `CavityFirstChild` neither receives nor updates `rows`. If a neighboring lid disappears, [BLGrowth12.inl:320](/media/rausa/4TB/SU2_Versions/SU2_NativeIntegrated/SU2_CFD/include/adaptation/native_bl3d/BLGrowth12.inl:320) subsequently calls `NodePoint` on its stale identity and aborts the event. Existing apex/override/extra entries can also become inconsistent with the replacement. **Fix:** pass the registry into cavity completion and reject transactions that invalidate registered column topology or identities, unless the transaction rebuilds and updates those registrations together.

2. **Major — V can re-register the non-columns that completion unregisters.** [BLEvent15.inl:149](/media/rausa/4TB/SU2_Versions/SU2_NativeIntegrated/SU2_CFD/include/adaptation/native_bl3d/BLEvent15.inl:149) removes registration only inside completion. A completed face can pass validity while still failing final shape thresholds. V then revisits that unregistered face at [BLVolume13.inl:275](/media/rausa/4TB/SU2_Versions/SU2_NativeIntegrated/SU2_CFD/include/adaptation/native_bl3d/BLVolume13.inl:275), and successful `FirstRowInsert`/`ApexRelocate` writes `rows.apex[key]` without checking its lifted vertices ([BLGrowth12.inl:492](/media/rausa/4TB/SU2_Versions/SU2_NativeIntegrated/SU2_CFD/include/adaptation/native_bl3d/BLGrowth12.inl:492), line 520). That false column enters V’s column classification and the continuation sidecar. **Fix:** enforce registration eligibility in the shared first-child helpers, covering every caller, rather than cleaning up only in `CompleteFirstRow`.

3. **Minor — Completion certificates mask later A3 violations.** Completion populates `certified`, then [CNativeBLPipeline3D.hpp:420](/media/rausa/4TB/SU2_Versions/SU2_NativeIntegrated/SU2_CFD/include/adaptation/CNativeBLPipeline3D.hpp:420) takes the audit snapshot without clearing those certificates. `FirstRowAudit` treats any subsequent change to those faces as certified, even if no certified operation occurred after the snapshot. Consequently, an unauthorized G/V change on a completed face can be reported as legitimate. **Fix:** clear `certified` immediately after taking the post-completion snapshot; preserve only certificates generated during the interval being audited. Apply the same correction to the cut harness.

4. **Minor — The cut harness does not exercise production’s new V behavior.** [native_first_row_dc13.inc:69](/media/rausa/4TB/SU2_Versions/SU2_NativeIntegrated/UnitTests/SU2_CFD/adaptation/fixtures/native_first_row_dc13.inc:69) adds completion, but its subsequent `PhaseV13` call retains the defaults: 1024 attempts, no near-wall ordering, no estimated scan, and no capped gate-scan skip. Production enables all four at [CNativeBLPipeline3D.hpp:362](/media/rausa/4TB/SU2_Versions/SU2_NativeIntegrated/SU2_CFD/include/adaptation/CNativeBLPipeline3D.hpp:362). A passing cut therefore cannot validate those production changes. **Fix:** add an explicit production-settings harness path with scoped restoration, and assert independent validity and registry consistency on its result.
