## A5 — exact surface-normal sign

The supplied profile reports broad `exact_predicates` at16.828% of full-native-window flat samples. In the rank-0 LBR decode (5,934 weighted stacks), only 28 samples have visible `SurfaceNormalSign` ancestry: 0.472% of all samples. The union of visible `SurfaceNormalSign`, `SurfaceOrientationSign`, `ExactDeterminant`, and `OrientationCentroidSign` ancestry is 322 samples (5.426% of samples); the 28 SurfaceNormalSign samples are 8.70% of that visible union. The difference between this visible set and the broad time group is other predicate work and/or samples whose ancestry does not expose one of those symbols; samples are not invocation counts or a direct decomposition of sampled time.

The observed runtime consumer is `MappedFacetReference::ComponentContainsFace`: affine components call `SurfaceNormalSign` at `Common/src/adaptation/CNativeAffineReference.cpp:3104-3109` (the exact call at 3107); curved components call `SurfaceOrientationSign` at 3109. The sampled `SurfaceNormalSign` stack ancestry reaches surface-union bounds (`TriangleUnion::PlaneUnion::Upper` / `QuerySurfaceUnionAtResolution` / `BoundPhysicalSurface`) and component containment. Other direct callers found are one-time affine setup/projection paths at `CNativeAffineReference.cpp:346-360, 560, 1936`; the observed consumer above is the relevant one. Kernel implementation: `CNativePredicates3D.cpp:308-318` always computes exact integer sign and increments `exact_orientations`; `SurfaceOrientationSign` already has a filtered long-double fast path and exact fallback at lines 319-352.

Existing sign tests are `UnitTests/Common/adaptation/CNativeAffineReference_tests.cpp:348-356` for orientation (scales 1e-300, 1, 1e300; same/reversed/degenerate), and 521-543 for exact ideal component queries plus SurfaceNormalSign positive/negative/zero cases at three scales. They do not record a real M6 operand corpus or benchmark a filtered-vs-exact sign equality/gain pair. Profile A's `actual_saved_surface_stage_rank0.json` records a real M6 checkpoint (303,823 input/donor cells, 305,000 output cells) and its adjacent owned/donor/parameter binaries preserve the stage mesh/state, but no captured `SurfaceNormalSign(normal, actual_triangle)` operand list or per-call sign receipt was found in the staged57 evidence. Those artifacts are suitable provenance/input state for a separately defined replay extraction, not already a predicate-input benchmark corpus. A direct reuse of `SurfaceOrientationSign` is not source-equivalent: it tests a dot product of two triangle normals, while `SurfaceNormalSign` tests a caller-supplied normal against the actual triangle; only the exact fallback pattern is analogous. A5 therefore has measured visibility but needs the real-input equality/gain evidence before implementation can be assessed.

A touched-point-only/lazy policy computation would not satisfy the current whole-state consumers unchanged. `PrepareMobility` explicitly computes the dimension census over every accepted boundary record (`CNativeRemesher3D.cpp:94-99`), while `BuildMobilityPolicy` produces globally complete `features`, `corners`, `sheets`, and `bodies` from all geometry edges/facets (above). Those are concrete complete-policy consumers; the preparation profile alone does not show that their state can be lazily omitted. No implementation or numerical changes were made for this review.
