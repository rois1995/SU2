# FP2-C3 fixture (c) search

The serial fixture derives the mixed cap from the S33 tent. The child with rim point 1 moved to z = -0.15 has height +0.15 at p's planar location. The other three child planes have height zero. The removed point is at +0.10, so the first cap is inward and the others are outward. The planar projection is the same fan and the reverse witnesses have vertical separation at most 0.25.

The bounded search for a full fan rejected by N1 used seed 2748039, independent Gaussian coordinate perturbations with scale uniformly distributed from 0.08 to 0.60, and batches of 512 samples. The eight-second exploration visited 10,752 samples. In 2,257 of them every old and proposed cell kept its orientation sign and all four new physical-face altitudes were at least 0.35. The floating separating-axis screen found no positive-volume overlap of a pair of new cells that did not share a face in that subset. This screen searched for volume overlaps before tests of the reverse geometry and exact conformity. The result does not establish that no such fan exists. The fixture uses the signed order's fallback with two exactly positive, overlapping tetrahedra that share only the keeper id, and calls the production N1 test directly.
