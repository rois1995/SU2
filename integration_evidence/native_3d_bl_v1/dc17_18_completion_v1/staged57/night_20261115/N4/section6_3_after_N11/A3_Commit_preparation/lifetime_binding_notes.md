# A3 Commit lifetime and binding notes

Scope: read-only mapping of PART1 §2.6 against the current Exchange candidate (validation pending). The Exchange candidate is active in source; the Commit draft below is unapplied. The mapping describes current aliases and consumers only.

- **Declaration order:** `private_parameters` is activated in EngineSetup first, then `result`/`stats`, `working`, Control `batch_bytes`, the three batch containers, and later the paired maximum IDs. Other Census, Queues, Exchange and Control locals interleave these fields. Current exact positions and source hashes are in `current_field_manifest.md`.
- **Canonical ownership:** `result` is `control.result` storage and `stats` aliases `result.stats`. `batch_bytes` belongs to `control.batch_bytes`. `private_parameters` and the observation counters belong to EngineSetup. The current Exchange `source` is constructed after the local `working` declaration with `(world, working, options.timings)`, so it binds that exact `Owned` object by reference. The active Directory and source are not Commit duplicates.
- **Proposed payload boundary, pending:** a proposed Commit holder contains exactly six optional values in original relative order: `working`, `batch_changed`, `batch_touched`, `batch_supports`, `maximum_node`, `maximum_cell`. Its `ContextValue` wrappers would stay at each current declaration point, preserving `working` before Exchange source construction and preserving the zero initializers of both ID marks. No reset, clear, byte charge, or body change is proposed. `batch_touched` and `batch_supports` currently accumulate changed-vertex/support records from batched repair; their consumers are `bytes()` and incremental admission sizing, and they are not cleared in the Adapt body.
- **Working/result flow:** `working` begins empty, receives `accepted` during initialization, is published through the current directory, can be swapped with a repartition/checkpoint mesh, and is finally swapped into `result.cells`. `batch_changed` collects IDs from batched accepted edits, feeds measurement/update, and clears at round end. `maximum_node`/`maximum_cell` are seeded/reduced high-water marks whose offsets feed fresh point/cell IDs; they are coupled to result edit order.
- **Accepted observations:** the current N12 lane census is Setup-owned print-only state. Trial/approval/commit calls and wall accepted counters observe the same real commit path but do not alter its decisions or edit records. They remain distinct from the proposed Commit payloads.
- **Captures and accounting:** `bytes`, `admit`, `finish`, `measure`, fixed-metric update, checkpoint helpers, and loop closures capture/read these locals by reference. The only raw `decltype` in Adapt is `decltype(checkpoint.blocked)`; no `typeid` use was found. Commit admission uses explicit `sizeof` terms (`Dependency`, `OwnedCell`, `Id`, `std::array<Id,4>`) plus container/capacity helpers. `bytes()` includes the same batch buffers and `working`; `admit()` also adds Exchange source/directory resident bytes. Preserve both the references and accounting boundary.
