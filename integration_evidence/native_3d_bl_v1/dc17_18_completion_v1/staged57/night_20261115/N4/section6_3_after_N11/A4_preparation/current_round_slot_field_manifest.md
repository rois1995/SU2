## Accepted lane observation additions

It is noncopyable; it has no declared move operations. `Counters::Approved` and `Counters::Commit` update print-only records. `lane_counters.Commit` is called after publication/update in the ordinary path (6208) and private batch path (6478); private batch retains the fixed slot metadata until then. No lane counter feeds selection or candidate acceptance in the inspected call paths.
