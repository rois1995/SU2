# A3 Exchange lifetime and binding notes

Scope: read-only lifetime map for PART1 §2.7 in the current A3 Queues candidate (validation pending). No field inventory here is a proposal to move or split any state.

- **Canonical objects and borrows:** `World` is the Adapt input and the sole collective/MPI context. `Directory` and `CavityMetricSource` retain references to it; they do not own another World. `working` is the one mutable partition, passed by reference to `CavityMetricSource::current`. `geometry` is the selected PlanarReference view and supplies `geometry.Parameters()` to Directory. The Adapt `EngineContext` also borrows the same world/accepted/original/geometry and control inputs. Their object identities are shared across Exchange calls.
- **Construction order and lifetime:** the Exchange declarations occur after `working` and the census/queue wrappers: `construction_claims`; active directory; metric source; checkpoint `ContextValue`; `retired_directory`; its `[&]` accumulator lambda; then byte/selection/seed helpers. `private_repair` is declared later, before communication wrappers and the loop. Reverse local destruction leaves `source` and `directory` alive through all later lambdas and finish/rollback paths; the context itself outlives Adapt locals. `Directory` is declared before `source`, while `source` holds `working`, which was declared earlier.
- **Constructor/destructor behavior:** `Directory` construction binds its `World&`, `track_changes`, and borrowed parameter-state pointer; `Directory::~Directory` is defaulted. `CavityMetricSource` construction binds `world`/`current` and initializes its `DonorImport bootstrap`; it has no user-declared destructor. `DonorImport` construction binds the world/timing inputs and its destructor is defaulted. `Directory::Build` later binds `parameter_geometry` from the parameter state.
- **Active/replaced directory protocol:** phase ownership constructs a pending directory for pending ownership, builds it, then accumulates old-directory statistics and swaps the new directory into the active pointer. Checkpoint capture can hold a separate dormant directory; rollback swaps it back and retires the attempted directory’s statistics. `construction_claims` tracks the pass state that accompanies the directory. These are linked active-state and historical-stat roles; `retired_directory` is not an alternate active Directory.
- **Source cavity binding:** the source’s `cavity` is a nullable, borrowed pointer to a vector of `OwnedCell`; `SetCavity` assigns and clears it around private repair handling. Its current partition reference remains `working`. `private_repair` gates whether operations use local methods or collective methods, so its true/false transitions are coupled to the local-versus-world behavior.
- **Lambda and type dependencies:** `retire_directory` captures the accumulator by reference. `finish`, `phase_ownership`, checkpoint helpers, import/measure helpers and wrappers capture the active Exchange aliases by `[&]`. The only raw `decltype` in this Adapt body is `decltype(checkpoint.blocked)` during rollback; no `typeid` use was found. Directory’s private `World&`, parameter-state pointer and parameter-geometry pointer are borrowed members; the metric source’s `World&`, `Owned&`, and cavity pointer are borrowed members.
- **Memory/accounting:** admissions include `source.ResidentBytes()` and `directory->ResidentBytes()` in multiple call paths. Directory’s standalone object size appears in the ownership/migration peak estimate as `sizeof(Directory)`. The `bytes()` closure counts queue/census/mesh objects but not `retired_directory` as a separate dynamic allocation; that accumulator is fixed `DirectoryStats` data. Preserve the active-vs-retired distinction when reading accounting reports.
- **No design inference:** this mapping only identifies existing references, local lifetimes, and source-supported communication roles. It does not decide the next ownership boundary or propose a new Exchange structure.
