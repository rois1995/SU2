## M7 local shell census

- D 5.1's local completeness check is implementable from existing cell/facet records without production changes. For each edge's local cavity, every cavity-cell face containing the edge must either have a second local incident cell or be a physical boundary face (`OwnedCell::physical[k].present`). For a vertex-star candidate, apply the analogous face test to every cavity-cell face containing the vertex. A face with no local mate and no physical marker is an interface cut and fails locally.
- For valid population accounting, report (a) number of globally unique visible edges, (b) sum of per-rank unique visible-edge counts (local visibility, allowing duplicate edges across ranks), and (c) number/share of globally unique edges whose complete shell is present on a rank. A global all-cell gather can form the unique denominator for this test-only census; do not use the local-visible sum as the global unique denominator. The same accounting should identify the rank owning the complete shell.
- `BoundaryParameterState::FacesOnVertex(Id)` returns a borrowed iterator range over `(facet, Face)` records (`Common/include/adaptation/CNativeBoundaryParameters3D.hpp:55-57, 109-112`; implementation `Common/src/adaptation/CNativeBoundaryParameters3D.cpp:539-540`). `Directory` borrows the boundary parameter pointer for its lifetime (`CNativeDistributed3D.hpp:182`); its collective `Build` and ownership model are declared at `:106-108`. An item-1 wall locality check must verify both full cavity-cell ownership and that every relevant wall face at every vertex of the changed wall faces has a local owner cell, using `FacesOnVertex` and local physical-face incidence. The cavity-only M7 test does not establish this stricter M9 condition.
