# FP2-A, build627

Correction 3 changes step D on the surface. A face with an above-limit feature chord keeps that chord's decision. Every other above-limit face selects its longest geometric edge, with the two point IDs breaking a tie. Refusal, a worker border or the strict floor at twice the distance limit leaves the selected edge for later work. The sweep output reports each refusal, deferrals and border insertions. Its per-pass row reports the faces left short and the live-cell incidence of the largest point stars after both surface steps. [Correction 3](../inputs/FP2_A_CORRECTION_3.md)

That file is outside Correction 3's reuse exception, so build626 OFF is historical and §11.2 remains on build627.
