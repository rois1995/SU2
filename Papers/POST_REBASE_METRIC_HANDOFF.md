## Production changes to integrate

1. **Stable metric intersections — f27bae8dcf.** CSolver::IntersectMetrics whitens in a commonly scaled A+B frame instead of nearly rank-one A. The generalized spectral maximum is unchanged mathematically. This stabilizes differently oriented, strongly anisotropic sensors and the shared helper used by BL composition/gradation. Independent 90-digit 2D/3D references cover condition 1e14 and scales 1e-150 to 1e150; errors around 4.6e-4/8.8e-4 fall to near machine precision on the tested rotated cases. Kernel cost increases about 7%/8% in the paired 2D/3D microbenchmark; this is a robustness tradeoff, not a speed optimization.

2. **Deterministic adaptation WLS accumulation — 3d57a82801.** Gradient and reused-geometry Hessian stencils accumulate neighbors in global-ID order. A small temporary buffer is reused per calling thread. Ordinary CFD gradient ordering is retained. The reversed-adjacency regression preserves adaptation derivatives exactly. Together with stable intersections, frozen M6 metric MPI discrepancy improves from approximately 7.37e-7 to 6.63e-15 in that campaign.

3. **Compensated primal sensor normalization — also 3d57a82801.** ComputeMetric reuses CAccurateSumBatch for the per-sensor normalization integrals; its protected arithmetic reduces partition-sensitive weak metric directions. Temporary payload is one passive scalar per owned point/sensor, about 1.47 MiB for serial two-sensor M6. AD builds retain active MPI sums. Noise-enabled RAE numerical MPI gates pass, but this does not validate noise filtering's preservation of flow features.

4. **Supported QR cubic wall fits — 6312fee370.** Grown, thin solid-wall stencils may add tangential/mixed cubic terms. Pure thin-direction cubic is omitted. Require sufficient samples, two residual degrees of freedom, numerical rank and condition bound <=1000; unsupported corrections retain quadratic fitting, then WLS if the quadratic fit fails. Reuse the existing two-ring communication; residual/noise calculations use the actual coefficient count. Examples: refined curved 2D transverse error 0.34688% -> 0.110208%; refined doubly curved/skewed 3D worst transverse error 12.8851% -> 6.48616%. Some coarse errors worsen; this is not universal high-order accuracy. It remains selected through NUM_METHOD_HESS=QUADRATIC_LEAST_SQUARES.

5. **QR symmetry support — 747f0ef6ea.** Permit direct QR on mirror-even primal scalar symmetry markers. Reuse existing normals and workspace to remove normal gradient and normal-tangential Hessian components while retaining normal-normal curvature. Manufactured oblique/orthogonal symmetry and frozen ONERA M6 checks cover 2D/3D and noise off/on. No extra ring, exchange or full-mesh buffer. Periodic, goal and differentiated QR remain rejected; arbitrary curved/nonorthogonal symmetry is not certified. Include CConfig, config_template and test changes, not just the QR header.

6. **Adaptation-only GG simplex recovery — 0eeb5a49ba.** Use exact P1 simplex gradients volume-averaged over complete owned triangle/tetrahedron stars in both recovery passes, followed by Hessian symmetrization. Accumulate geometry and field differences deterministically by global ID. Supported boundary affine gradient errors fall from 18.18/121.04 in 2D/3D to about 1.85e-12/3.33e-12; scaled affine Hessians are below 3.3e-13. Curved wall-interior Hessian maximum-component errors fall roughly 45x/105x on the finest manufactured 2D/3D examples. Boundary bias and corner errors remain. Ordinary convective/viscous callers default to the legacy operator. Periodic/goal and mixed/invalid/incomplete stars retain their prior paths. Include computeHessians, CAdapSensors and CSolver opt-in call sites; changing only the kernel does not enable adaptation recovery.

7. **Reduce repeated GG simplex geometry — 7cfc984277.** Evaluate geometry/contributions once per volume cell per recovery pass and scatter in global-ID order to owned points. Temporary sums, volumes and validity flags preserve whole-star fallback before writes. No permanent geometry cache or new MPI exchange. Paired M6 recovery median 0.57025 -> 0.39355 seconds, about 31% less than the preceding corrected GG implementation. Explicit maximum scratch payload is 11.52 MiB in the tested two-sensor M6 pass. The corrected recovery still costs substantially more than legacy GG; the traversal is serial and full hybrid scaling is untested. Reassociation changes low bits, so only the measured MPI/error tolerances, not old/new bitwise equivalence, are claimed.

8. **Supported coupled QR quartic wall terms — also 7cfc984277.** Extend supported thin two-ring solid-wall fits with selected tangential/mixed quartics containing at most one thin-coordinate factor. Retain the sample/rank/condition gates and cubic -> quadratic -> WLS fallback. At n=32 on the manufactured curved/skewed 3D layer, wall-interior relative normal-curvature error 2.0761% -> 1.0036%. Matched tested 2D outputs are unchanged because those stencils cannot support the coupled basis. The smaller tangential-only variant failed convergence and is excluded. Paired M6 QR recovery median increases about 1.75%; frozen M6 fields themselves are unchanged. No complete degree-four or additional normal-layer accuracy claim.

9. **Remove duplicate steady metric construction — f4017c9d70.** RunAdaptationLoop consumes the metric just completed by Postprocess through remesher->Remesh, instead of rebuilding it in RemeshFromMetric. Public RemeshFromMetric/AdaptMesh retain refresh behavior for potentially changed solutions. The existing unsteady window path is preserved. Regression counts one metric build per mesh and a fresh build for the explicit public path at 1/2/4 ranks. Matched adapted mesh, sidecar and all saved fields are identical. Structural saving: one full metric build per steady replacement, three -> two builds in the tested one-cycle workflow. Integrate into the other branch's latest driver without replacing its lifecycle/output/worker changes.

10. **Reduce repeated native graded-complexity work — 34e335842c.** Skip tensor recomposition when every transported demand is already satisfied. Warm-start corner-update complexity solves from the previous accepted scale while retaining safeguarded brackets, bounds and 1e-6 complexity tolerance. Reuse the last evaluated accepted field, with refresh for an endpoint that was not last evaluated. Add tensor-update/work counters. The paired frozen four-rank RAE GG metric median drops 17.2081 -> 6.25507 seconds, 63.65%, with 78 -> 29 complexity trials. This checkpoint alone retains 104 capped nodal violations; integrate its following convergence fix too.

11. **Active-stencil nodal gradation and demand-based stopping — d980963c67.** First round visits all points; later expensive stencils run only for changed tensors, their neighbors and prior attempted corrections. Compare changes after halo exchange and preserve synchronous previous tensors/global-ID order. Retry attempted corrections even if clipping leaves a tensor unchanged. Stop only when no rank attempts a correction at the existing 1e-7 demand threshold; replace the old relative-change eigensolve. Safety cap increases 80 -> 256; it is a maximum, not a mandatory count or universal convergence guarantee. Keep full eligibility scans, halos and one global MAX reduction per round. The paired four-rank RAE GG field has 104 -> 0 nodal violations, accepted rounds 80 capped -> 127 converged, 52.13% fewer expensive stencil visits and median 5.82788 -> 5.06883 seconds, 13.02% less than the preceding optimized executable. More synchronization rounds can increase MPI costs. All 18 frozen GG/WLS/QR combinations at targets 12000/60000 and 1/2/4 ranks pass nodal constraints. Include the remote-demand regression and 2D/3D bounded-target tests.

These successive metric performance measurements come from separate matched campaigns; do not add their percentages or present them as a whole-CFD speedup.

## Preserve the geometric BL contract during integration

Metric_* and immutable donor data remain sensor-only. Interpolate on original donor connectivity, then evaluate original-wall BL constraints at the actual candidate/quadrature location. Composition is an intersection retaining finer normal/tangential sensor demands and coupling. Keep main's geometric integration, fade and sidecar ownership. No post-rebase production change propagates composite BL tensors, hard-resets wall-normal sizes or restores the rejected Schur forcing.

Nodal sensor transport is the implemented certificate. P1 interpolation between vertices and geometric sensor-plus-BL transport remain uncertified. Smoothstep/log fade can violate global hgrad even with an exactly uniform sensor when the prescribed transition band is too narrow; local BL growth is not a substitute for a global composed-field certificate. Native 3D remeshing and 3D geometric BL are not validated by the tetrahedral sensor-recovery tests. General mixed meshes, periodic QR and convective/viscous transfer remain follow-ups. Noise stays opt-in with default ADAP_HESSIAN_NOISE=0.
