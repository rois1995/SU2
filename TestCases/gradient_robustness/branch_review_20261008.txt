Recommended order and supported effects
---------------------------------------
1. Scalar MUSCL correctness: fix_scalar_muscl / PR #2956.
   Fork and upstream have the same head b2234a5e1e44d22a4dc045f71ecde5371766743a.
   Open, not draft, not merged; absent from our baseline.
   https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2956

   Confirmed baseline mechanisms:
   - CommonPreprocessing stops recomputing point limiters after
     LIMITER_ITER, but CUpwScalarBase also sets the applied limiter
     type to NONE afterward. Freezing therefore removes limiting.
   - VAN_ALBADA_EDGE goes through the point-limiter dispatcher and
     aborts for scalar equations. The scalar flux also suppresses
     the flow's edge limiter when reconstructing carrying velocity.
   - A separate scalar reconstruction gradient is computed when
     the global flow needs one even if MUSCL_TURB/SPECIES is NO.
   - Scalar face reconstruction lacks lower/upper admissibility bounds.
   - Bounded correction uses mass flux for nonconservative variables
     instead of the matching upwind volume flux, and lacks the
     conservative-variable density division in its diagonal.
     Sliding donor corrections are not weighted like their fluxes.

   Reviewed fixes preserve the last point limiter, dispatch edge
   limiting in the shared reconstruction helper, skip unused
   reconstruction gradients, pass scalar bounds, match the bounded
   residual/Jacobian coefficients and weight sliding donors.
   Reaches SA, SST and species through the shared scalar kernel;
   inspect transition/flamelet/heat/pressure-scalar configurations
   when adopting, not only the turbulence callers.

   Expected effect: retain shock/large-gradient control after freezing,
   allow second-order turbulence with the edge limiter, and prevent
   finite negative/out-of-range face k or omega. This is a real route
   to better robustness; it does not prove higher order on mixed grids.
   First-order fallback adds local dissipation and needs monitoring.

   Author's evidence reports SST NACA0012 MUSCL at 10 degrees changing
   from 10000 iterations without convergence to 527; density-scaled
   bounded SA reproducing the density-one drag; frozen-limiter and
   edge-limiter comparisons. These are case-specific reported results.
   https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2956#issuecomment-5960636166
   All 37 checks returned for the reviewed head were successful,
   including serial/MPI/hybrid, AD, unit and sanitizer jobs.

   Important limit independently checked: the bounds fallback uses
   out*phi1st + (1-out)*phi. Comparisons do not detect NaN, and 0*Inf
   is NaN. It handles finite out-of-range states, not arbitrary
   nonfinite states. Test nonfinite rejection and avoid invalid masked
   arithmetic before describing this as general positivity protection.
   Our probe is IEEE scalar arithmetic, not the full flux/AD kernel.
   The bounds of SA_NEG must keep its allowed negative values.

2. Compressible internal-energy update guard: PR #2942.
   Upstream fix_underrelaxation_factor head d875b761191b1d3193b163fbdcaa0851c851cf10.
   Open, not draft, not merged; absent by direct baseline inspection.
   https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2942

   CEulerSolver::ComputeUnderRelaxationFactor subtracts
   |rho*u|^2/2 from rho*E; it must subtract |rho*u|^2/(2*rho),
   separately with old and proposed density. This can freeze valid
   updates when density is away from one.
   Algebraic example: rho=10, rho*u=20, rho*E=30 and energy update=0.1.
   Baseline internal energy is -170 and relaxation is zero;
   corrected internal energy is 10 and relaxation is one for a
   maximum update fraction of 0.2. This is an isolated calculation,
   not a full nonlinear CFD test.
   Expected effect: remove artificial update freezing; residual histories
   can change widely. It does not change the spatial residual formula.
   All 38 returned checks for the reviewed head were successful.

3. Flow limiter fixes: fix_limiters / PR #2959.
   Fork/upstream same head 43d0afdd2d2942b30167d84eaccabfe1ec7bd227.
   Open, not draft, not merged; absent from our baseline.
   https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2959

   R4 computes Dp=abs(delta) but cubes signed delta in its denominator.
   Replacing delta^3 with Dp^3 restores overshoot/undershoot symmetry.
   Our compiled extracted LimiterHelpers reproduced 13 failures in
   the branch's 50 symmetry/reference checks on our baseline and
   zero on the fix. At proj=delta=1 and zero epsilon, R4 is 0.875;
   at proj=delta=-1 baseline is 1.1666667 and fixed is 0.875.
   This confirms the formula defect, not a general CFD speedup.

   WALL_DISTANCE is also requested without a turbulence solver;
   otherwise laminar limiter users can receive zero wall distances
   and first-order reconstruction throughout. Zero-width geometric
   limiters are rejected; NEMO limiter output names are corrected.
   Older fix_nishikawa_r4 duplicates the R4 idea; use the newer
   fix_limiters candidate rather than applying both.

   CI is not entirely green: MPI and hybrid jobs fail reference
   comparisons. Inspected logs show flatplate_supersonic (MPI) and
   jones_turbocharger_restart (MPI/hybrid); Jones hybrid difference
   is 0.000177 with tolerance 0.0001. Cause is not established here.
   Resolve against a matched baseline before refreshing references.
   https://github.com/su2code/SU2/actions/runs/37310253437

4. Krylov and parallel linear algebra corrections.
   Fork fix_krylov_solvers head eb5d695c5f86eafa2bc3ab6acf185f792e97d595.
   https://github.com/rois1995/SU2/tree/fix_krylov_solvers
   Missing fixes preserve relative tolerance across restarted FGMRES,
   limit recycled FGCRODR vectors when the next solve shrinks,
   avoid an update with no new Krylov direction after a failed
   orthogonalization, validate deflation size, and correct monitoring
   frequency and a quantized recycled-vector sizing error.
   The branch includes unit cases; these were reviewed, not run here.
   Expected effect: correct stopping and prevent solver corner-case
   failures, especially with inexact/restarted implicit solves.
   Only relevant paths change; ordinary nonrestarted FGMRES is not
   evidence of benefit from the restarted-tolerance correction.

   Fork fix_linear_solver_parallel head 8d96b78d70d3c6b42954e4baac443c240c5e744c.
   https://github.com/rois1995/SU2/tree/fix_linear_solver_parallel
   ILU level scheduling is restricted to owned rows, since halo
   factors do not exist. Identity preconditioning exchanges its output
   halos before matrix products. Missing in baseline. Tests include
   MPI identity and MPI2 x OpenMP2 level-scheduled ILU configurations.
   Expected effect: valid distributed Krylov/preconditioner data,
   not higher spatial accuracy. Requires actual partitioned testing.

   Upstream LINELET PR #2899 is also open and absent by source review.
   Its title and older body disagree about scope: the current patch
   DOES change geometry and matrix code. It uses a collective-safe
   computed flag and allocates linelet work arrays even on ranks with
   no lines. Such ranks otherwise can access unallocated vectors.
   This is relevant to partitions away from anisotropic wall layers.
   https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2899
   No independent MPI reproduction or timing was performed here.

5. Turbulence limiter scaling: fork fix_scalar_limiter_eps.
   Head ae77daf13ea59eddc0c71681edf44541211d737e; not in baseline
   or the reviewed fix_scalar_muscl head. It is a separate experiment.
   https://github.com/rois1995/SU2/tree/fix_scalar_limiter_eps
   Divide projections and field differences by max(abs(phi),phi_inf)
   for turbulence/transition point limiters with absolute epsilon;
   Wang stays range-relative. Flow/species remain unchanged.
   Expected effect: more consistent limiting across variable magnitude
   and units, especially small nu_tilde and very large omega.
   Treat this as a numerical change needing verification, not merely
   a coding correction. Near-zero free-stream/reference values, wall
   layers, nondimensionalization and limiter activation need tests.
   The new reference value is not registered as a preaccumulation
   input in the patch; check sensitivities when that reference is active.
   This does not address the shared edge limiter's absolute 1e-6
   regularization in numerics/util.hpp. Scaling the point path alone
   does not establish scale invariance of every limiter.

Other relevant candidates, conditionally prioritized

- SA wall functions, PR #2957 / origin/fix_sa_wall_function.
  Merged upstream as e28f35a11041bb8f7d3b2eb95a5dcaa2087fe19d,
  but absent from our baseline by direct diff. Starts the Newton
  inversion at the off-wall neighbor, at least the target nu_t,
  rather than a drifting unconstrained wall value.
  Expected effect: avoid failed inversion/divergence for wall-function
  restarts. Relevant only when wall functions are enabled.
  https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2957

- SST energy consistency, PR #2946, head 37dcfedd0e07553af04066473fb386fb6af1516c.
  Draft/open. Convective fluxes, sound speeds, eigenvectors, boundary
  states and sources must treat k in the total energy consistently.
  Reviewed scalar and SIMD patches add this propagation and tests.
  Potentially important for high Mach/high turbulence; broad physics
  and AD reach, so test independently rather than silently bundling.
  PR #2948 is stacked on it and also changes selectable SST variants,
  sustaining ambient values and optional wall omega clipping.
  Keep model-definition changes separate from robustness bug fixes.
  https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2946
  https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2948

- Flow MUSCL face validation and moving-grid fixes, PRs #2943–#2945.
  Draft/open, stacked on #2942; broad older PR #2941 is CLOSED,
  UNMERGED and superseded. Scalar pressure/density checks are moved
  ahead of thermodynamic evaluation. This patch alone does not fix
  every arithmetic-mask issue in the ideal-gas SIMD reconstruction.
  HLLC moving-frame flux and finite-difference Jacobian tests (#2943)
  matter for actual mesh motion; L2Roe/LMRoe/general-gas 3D wave
  strengths (#2944) matter on oblique faces. Standard Roe is excluded
  from that defect. Incompressible moving-grid FDS/JST/Lax Jacobians
  (#2945) correct repeated grid terms without changing the residual.
  Remeshing between stationary meshes is distinct from ALE motion.
  https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2943
  https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2944
  https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2945

- Rotational periodicity, fork fix_periodic_rotation / PR #2961.
  Head 9de9fd0b8298cc17a6e0fe4df8c2a4f74f8a3159, open/non-draft,
  absent by baseline source inspection. Transform implicit blocks
  on both sides, rotate coarse-level velocity, and exchange limiter
  bounds/increments in the matching frame instead of rotating
  component limiter factors like a vector. All 37 returned checks
  successful. Relevant to rotational periodic adapted meshes;
  separate from the still unsupported periodic QR fallback.
  https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2961

- Stretched-mesh multigrid, upstream feature_mg_linear_prolong / #2931.
  Head 537b3c722d1a095179ed700b749b753f32b94f1e, open/non-draft.
  Proposes linear prolongation, anisotropy-aware partitioning, layer
  agglomeration fixes, coarse turbulence handling, all-level viscous
  correction, residual-based CFL and local first order at wall ends.
  The viscous Jacobian uses a clipped effective projected distance,
  so it is an approximation requiring convergence tests.
  These are promising mechanisms, not established universal gains.
  https://github.com/su2code/SU2/pull/2931

  Independently compiled its separate invertLeastSquaresMatrix.
  Full-rank diagonal matrices for stencil AR=1,1000,10000 pass;
  AR=20000,100000,1000000 fail and give constant prolongation.
  det/(trace/2)^2 = 4*AR^-2/(1+AR^-2)^2 and cutoff=1e-8.
  This is an intentional conditioning fallback, but loses the linear
  enhancement in precisely our extreme anisotropy range. Our shared
  gradient QR does not reach this separate implementation. Reuse an
  equilibrated robust fit if exploring it.
  Eight returned regression jobs fail; unit/build/sanitizer jobs pass.
  Inspected serial/MPI logs contain broad reference mismatches, not
  proof of divergence. Resolve changed defaults/operators and baselines
  before adoption, rather than updating references automatically.
  https://github.com/su2code/SU2/actions/runs/37186043098

- Upstream fix_bcgstab, eaed58fb9c8c98893dbe4386299b7a7b895345ff.
  Returns the best recorded iterate when COMM_FULL detects the last
  residual is worse. The same proposal appears inside #2931.
  More vectors/copies; no accuracy/convergence-time guarantee, and
  test recurrence breakdown/early exit as well as capped iterations.
  Do not apply twice.
  https://github.com/su2code/SU2/tree/fix_bcgstab

Next steps
----------
1. On codex/cfd-mesh-robustness, isolate the internal-energy correction
   and shared scalar MUSCL correctness fixes in separate commits.
   Recompute branch test references from our matched baseline; do not
   copy upstream references for a different integration.
2. Verify freeze/restart behavior, finite/nonfinite face bounds,
   SA_NEG admissibility, bounded density scaling, and flow/turbulence
   limiter combinations. Compare serial and truly partitioned MPI;
   check representative field/geometry derivatives with limiter
   preaccumulation, not just the QR helper.
3. Validate R4 plus WALL_DISTANCE/config guards; reproduce the failing
   CI comparisons against the parent before accepting new references.
4. RANS SA/SST on identical regular, stretched, skewed and curved
   meshes: record flow AND turbulence residuals, limiter/fallback
   activity, forces, skin friction and wall heat flux. Require matched
   convergence before comparing spatial errors; run a refinement study.
   First fix solver correctness, then isolate QR and limiter-scaling
   effects. The existing 12 QR CFD runs did not establish an accuracy
   improvement and most reached the iteration cap.
5. Apply distributed linear algebra fixes where relevant, then explore
   CFL/agglomeration/prolongation independently with total nonlinear
   work, linear work and time. Avoid mixing SST model changes into the
   same accuracy experiment.
6. Keep one compile job and one-thread sequential cases by default,
   using low scheduling priority; use MPI2 only for focused ownership
   checks while other workers share the machine.

Reporting for subsequent work: state changes completed and tested,
observed effects separately from expected effects, and concrete next
steps. Preserve negative results and unresolved checks.

No new full CFD, RANS, ALE, remeshing or full adjoint campaign was run
for this branch review. Main risks are cross-branch physics/default
changes, scalar nonfinite handling, multigrid anisotropic fallback,
distributed ownership, and interpreting unconverged residuals as
accuracy or interpreting changed CI references as proven defects.
