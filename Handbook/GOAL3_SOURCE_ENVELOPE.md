## Implementation direction

- Use the boundary of the actual frozen ORIGINAL volume, not automatically the
  immutable initial geometry mesh. One common generated coordinate and original
  sensor copy per source vertex; canonical face diagonals must match globally.
- Derive the required coverage from the selected geometry authority and mapping
  envelope. Do not equate the maximum physical approximation tolerance with a
  uniform source width. Numerical mapping and physical approximation are distinct.
- For joint affine mapping, a bounded shared collar is viable on the complete saved
  nonconvex annulus. Prepare once per source/reference version; certify positivity,
  conformity, complete envelope coverage and memory/work before publication. Use
  current collective failure/atomic import semantics and boundary-only indexes;
  no whole-volume gather or query-time geometry negotiation.
- Preserve qualified analytic collars where their existing coverage contracts hold.
  Mixed/curved junctions still need a coherent actual envelope. The broad1e-4 M6
  column experiment is rejected; no extra collar-mesher/fallback mechanism is yet
  justified. Source coverage and production mapped-feature semantics must connect
  before another positive full oblique attempt.
- Original physical P1 has priority. Extension tensors are admitted convex original
  trace combinations; actual-query BL/finer demands/fade and stage freezing remain.
  Sensor support, field version, geometry binding and edge interval tracing must
  agree for local/private/remote queries and N/M migration. Do not replace them
  with candidate tags or an unannounced nearest-face value.
