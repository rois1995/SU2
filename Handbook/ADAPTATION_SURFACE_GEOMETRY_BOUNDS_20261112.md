one facet; failed direct bounds may fall back to BoundPhysicalSurface. Cross-patch
faces discover NearbySurfaceFacets using the current triangle enclosure, then filter

subdivision returns tightened leaf maxima. These are valid bounds but their numerical
magnitudes need not be equally sharp for different parent/child queries.
