constructs a geometric gap model on a physical tangent plane, treats model curves
separately, and intersects their constraints. §4.2 extends the surface metric with

parameters to avoid repeated projections. §5.1 evaluates surface error using an

parameters in our diagnosis. They establish neither a BL first-height law nor
equivalence to our strict certification. Our existing no-CAD authority already
